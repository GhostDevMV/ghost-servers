How It Works & Service Policy

  1. Consultation: We discuss your server goals, expected player count, and features to choose the best hosting plan.
  2. Step 1 Payment (Hosting Fee): You transfer the required host plan amount. We purchase the server directly for your project.
  3. Development: We build and configure the server while sharing continuous progress updates and screenshots.
  4. Step 2 Payment (Development Fee): Once the server is 100% ready and tested, you pay the remaining development fee (10€–20€).
  5. Handover: Full ownership and control of the server and host account are transferred to you.

*Note: Initial hosting payments are non-refundable as they cover direct third-party server rental fees. Unclaimed completed projects will remain developer property.
